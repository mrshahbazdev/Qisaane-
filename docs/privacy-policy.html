Qisaane — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

Qisaane is an offline and property register for landlords and property dealers. It is designed so that your data never leaves your computer unless you explicitly choose to share it on your own network.

What the app stores

Everything you type into Qisaane — property and unit records, tenant names, CNIC numbers, phone numbers, amounts, bills, receipts, deposits, meter readings and expenses — is saved only on your computer, inside an encrypted database (SQLCipher). The encryption key is protected by your operating system's credential store.

What the app does NOT do

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • No data is uploaded to any server operated by us or by anyone else.
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • No internet connection is needed for any feature.

Optional LAN sharing

If you turn on "Share on this WiFi", the app serves your data to other PCs on your own local network only, protected by an access code. Nothing is sent over the internet. You can turn it off at any time and the data becomes unreachable from other devices.

Backups

Backup files you export are written where you choose (e.g. a USB drive). They are your responsibility — keep them safe.

Your control

You can delete all data at any time from Settings → "Reset — delete all data". Uninstalling the app removes the program; the encrypted database in your user-data folder can be deleted manually.
